# Pre-tests before v3: settling and exposure

Generated by `lvl1/make_packet_plan_v3split.py` (reasons in its docstring). Take the packets in order with

```bash
DRY_RUN=1 PLAN_DIR=lvl1/packet_plan_pre bash daq_scripts/run_plan.sh
PLAN_DIR=lvl1/packet_plan_pre bash daq_scripts/run_plan.sh
```

Plan settings (plan.json `run` / `env`; a packet's own `env` wins): `N_REF_SETS=1`, `N_SETS=3`, `N_POST_SETS=0`, `IMAGE_TOML=image_params/tc1/img32.toml`. Changes go to: acm101.

**Order matters: not shuffled.** Two questions, each read off a handful of packets:
* **Settling.** The same setting imaged right after the change and after a 10 / 20 / 30 min hold (the hold is `PAUSE_S`, the wait after every trace, so the 12 new traces are spread over it). If lambda or sigma_e at the long hold differs from the short hold by more than the nominal scatter, the packets image a transient and every later plan needs a hold. The nominal packet with the same hold is the control.
* **Exposure.** lambda against EXPOSURE (0, 300, 900 s; the 32-row readout itself is 573 s) at nominal and at VSUB +15 V: the slope is the dark-current rate, the intercept the charge made by the readout. If the slope is small the exposure pairs of the dark-charge map can be dropped.
Needed on the DAQ machine: `image_params/tc1/img32.toml`, `img32_exp300.toml`, `img32_exp900.toml` (configs/image_params_*.toml).
Not checked: `process_packet.py` on images with an exposure; a `time.sleep` of ~2 min in the cdaq queue (the holds).

| session | block | packets | time |
|---|---|---|---|
| P | nominal | 6 | 2.2 h |
| P | settling | 5 | 2.6 h |
| P | exposure | 4 | 1.7 h |
| | **total** | **15** | **6.6 h** |

Checks: every configuration passes the windows of configs/limits.toml (`reviewed = false`), the relationship rules with a 0.5 V margin, clock swing >= 1.5 V, and is reset-safe (any one or two pins back at nominal). All 22 files replayed line by line; largest single step 0.5 V (VSUB 5 V).
