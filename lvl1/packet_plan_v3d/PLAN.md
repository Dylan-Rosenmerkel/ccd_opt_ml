# v3 dark-charge add-on: one-at-a-time sweeps and exposure pairs

Generated by `lvl1/make_packet_plan_v3split.py` (reasons in its docstring). Take the packets in order with

```bash
DRY_RUN=1 PLAN_DIR=lvl1/packet_plan_v3d bash daq_scripts/run_plan.sh
PLAN_DIR=lvl1/packet_plan_v3d bash daq_scripts/run_plan.sh
```

Plan settings (plan.json `run` / `env`; a packet's own `env` wins): `N_REF_SETS=1`, `N_SETS=1`, `N_POST_SETS=0`, `IMAGE_TOML=image_params/tc1/img32.toml`, `L1_STRIP=0`. Changes go to: acm101.

What the noise set (joint Sobol, 32-row image) cannot give for lambda: single effects and the rate. A settings-only measurement (the traces added nothing to the settings for lambda in v2), so one trace set each side, no strips, the 32-row image. Groups with a real range only: VSUB -10..+25 V, VDD -2..+2 V, VR -1..+1 V, SW_L -2..+0 V, H1_L -1.3..+0.2 V, H2_L -1.5..+0.2 V, H3_L -1.3..+0.2 V, H_swing -0.8..+0.5 V. The V lows, V swing and TG are left out: the rules leave them 0.5 V or less, below what the label noise resolves.
**Exposure pairs** (10 settings + nominal twice): the setting again with EXPOSURE 600 s right after its EXPOSURE 0 packet. The difference is the dark-current rate at that setting; what is left is charge made by the readout. Drop them (`--exposure-pairs 0`) if the pre-tests show no rate.
Rejected joint points: 19. Needed on the DAQ machine: `image_params/tc1/img32.toml`, `image_params/tc1/img32_exp600.toml`.
Take it only after the pre-tests: if lambda settles slowly, add a hold (`PAUSE_S` in plan.json `env`).

Checks: every configuration passes the windows of configs/limits.toml (`reviewed = false`), the relationship rules with a 0.5 V margin, clock swing >= 1.5 V, and is reset-safe (any one or two pins back at nominal). All 94 files replayed line by line; largest single step 1 V (VSUB 5 V).
