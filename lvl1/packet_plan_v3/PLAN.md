Why these groups: **VSUB** substrate: depletion, dark current (the strongest voltage effect on lambda in v2, amplifier-dependent); **VDD** output drain: amplifier operating point; **VR** reset drain; **RG_H** reset-gate high; **RG_L** reset-gate low; **SW_L** summing-well low; **V1_L** vertical phase 1 low: surface dark current, clock-induced charge; **V2_L** vertical phase 2 low; **V3_L** vertical phase 3 low; **V_swing** vertical clock swing (high - low; the highs were never varied before): with the lows, separates level from swing; **H1_L** horizontal phase 1 low: serial clock-induced charge; **H2_L** horizontal phase 2 low; **H3_L** horizontal phase 3 low; **H_swing** horizontal clock swing (high - low; never varied before); **TG** transfer gate (high = low); bounds the V lows from below and the H lows from above.

## The order of the changes, ramps, and the way back

manual_packet.sh sends a changes file line by line. Every file is written so that every intermediate configuration passes the windows and the relationship rules: large changes are ramped (VSUB in steps of at most 5 V, every other voltage in steps of its max_step), all pins moving together, and within a step the pins are sent in a rule-safe order (a pin may appear several times; the last value holds). Every V and H clock keeps a swing (high - low) of at least 1.5 V, and every relationship rule a packet touches keeps a margin of at least 0.5 V (the rail readback tolerance is 0.05 V).

**The way back.** Each `<id>.txt` (and `<id>_ref.txt`) has an `<id>_undo.txt` (`<id>_ref_undo.txt`): the way back to nominal, ramped and in a rule-safe order. manual_packet.sh's reset reloads the module's .bcf, which restores the pins in an order we do not control (it can put a TG high briefly below its TG low after a TG change); sending the undo file just before the reset makes the reset a no-op. manual_packet.sh does not do this yet.

## Before taking it

* `image_params/tc1/img32.toml` on the DAQ machine (as v2);
* record the CCD temperature per packet if a readout exists (lambda drifts);
* the voltage windows (`reviewed = false`).
