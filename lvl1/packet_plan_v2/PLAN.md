# Level-1 packet plan, version 2

Generated by `lvl1/make_packet_plan_v2.py` (rules and reasons in its docstring). Take each packet with

```bash
N_REF_SETS=3 N_SETS=3 IMAGE_TOML=image_params/tc1/img32.toml PACKET_ID=L2_NN CHANGES_FILE=lvl1/packet_plan_v2/L2_NN.txt bash daq_scripts/manual_packet.sh
```

(with `DRY_RUN=1` first). **Take the packets in this order.** Or let `daq_scripts/run_plan.sh` take them one after the other: it reads these settings from plan.json, skips finished packets, and stops on an error or when a file `STOP` appears in the records directory.

**Voltage windows:** configs/limits.toml has `reviewed = false`: the voltage changes use its PLACEHOLDER windows and are provisional.

## What changed from v1, and why

* **Joint design.** Every training packet moves all 12 knob groups at once, to a point of a scrambled Sobol sequence in the box below (timing on a log scale). v1 changed one knob at a time to one value, so each setting was seen about once. Distinct levels per knob in session D: delayInt 48, delay_H 48, delayRGH 45, VDD 48, VR 44, VSUB 48, RG_H 44, RG_L 46, SW_L 39, H_low 45, V_low 41, TG 38.
* **Nominal every 7 packets** (v1: 4 in 32), to track the CCD's drift (lambda fell ~20 % over the 2026-11-02 night) and measure the label noise.
* **3 replicates** of design points from the first half of session D, in its second half.
* **Session E** is a separate Sobol sequence: the prospective test, as v1's session C.
* **Reference not always nominal** (2026-11-04): 21 packets of session D take their reference at the previous packet's settings (`<id>_ref.txt`), so within-packet changes go up and down (with a nominal reference 96 % of the clear sigma_e changes go up).
* **Traces bracket the image**: 3 new sets before the strip and image, 2 after.
* **Session F, the funnel test**: trace-only screens at new Sobol points; the models trained on D+E shortlist the best from their traces with two rankings side by side, with the screen's reference trace and without it (screens compared two at a time, then sorted) (`lvl1/funnel_shortlist.py`, weights 1:0, 0.5:0.5, 0:1 of sigma_e:lambda); 6 shortlisted settings are imaged next to 5 random controls drawn now.
* Points that break a rule are redrawn (D: 0 rejected, E: 0).

Why these parameters: **delayInt** CDS integration window: white noise falls with a longer window, slow noise grows; the strongest noise knob; **delay_H** horizontal clock hold: charge transfer and horizontal clock-induced charge; **delayRGH** reset-gate hold before the pedestal window: settling of the reset; **VDD** output drain: the amplifier's operating point (noise, gain); **VR** reset drain: reset level and its noise; **RG_H** reset-gate high rail; **RG_L** reset-gate low rail; **SW_L** summing-well low rail; **H_low** horizontal clock low rails: clock-induced charge; **V_low** vertical clock low rails: clock-induced charge, dark current at the surface; **TG** transfer gate (high = low); **VSUB** substrate voltage: depletion, dark current.

## Packet procedure (daq_scripts/manual_packet.sh, 2026-11-04)

1. reset to nominal and read back (packets with `<id>_ref.txt`: then the reference settings and 2 settling traces); flush;
2. the reference level-1 strip, then 3 reference trace sets (4 channels, each board separately, 2 s pause after every trace); flush;
3. (from a non-nominal reference: a reset to nominal) the changes; 2 settling traces (not used);
4. 3 trace sets at the new settings; the new level-1 strip; flush;
5. the image (32 rows, `image_params/tc1/img32.toml`), 2 s pause; standalone; 2 more trace sets at the new settings; flush; reset to nominal and read back.

Screen packets (session F, `SCREEN=1`): reset; flush; 1 reference trace set; flush; the changes; 2 settling traces; 2 new trace sets; reset. No strips, no image.

**Safeguards** (manual_packet.sh, CHECK_TRACES=1): after the reference and after the new traces the script waits for both boards, checks every board-101 trace (src/check_trace_files.py) and retakes damaged ones; more than 5 damaged traces in a packet restart it from the reset as `<id>_r1` (the stopped attempt's record says "restarted" and is skipped by make_manual_manifest.py). On the 2026-11-02 data (~8 % of the traces damaged, mostly a few per packet; L1_37/40/41 lost two thirds) about 1 packet in 10 would restart.

## Before taking it

* copy `configs/image_params_32rows.toml` to `<CDAQ_DIR>/image_params/tc1/img32.toml` on the DAQ machine (32 rows: the dark-current part of lambda ~3x better than with 16);
* record the CCD temperature per packet (the drift of lambda), if a readout exists;
* the voltage windows (`reviewed = false`).
