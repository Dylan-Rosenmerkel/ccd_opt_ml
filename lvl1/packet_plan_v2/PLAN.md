## What changed from v1, and why

* **Joint design.** Every training packet moves all 12 knob groups at once, to a point of a scrambled Sobol sequence in the box below (timing on a log scale). v1 changed one knob at a time to one value, so each setting was seen about once. Distinct levels per knob in session D: delayInt 48, delay_H 48, delayRGH 45, VDD 48, VR 44, VSUB 48, RG_H 44, RG_L 46, SW_L 39, H_low 45, V_low 41, TG 38.
* **Nominal every 7 packets** (v1: 4 in 32), to track the CCD's drift (lambda fell ~20 % over the 2026-11-02 night) and measure the label noise.
* **3 replicates** of design points from the first half of session D, in its second half.
* **Session E** is a separate Sobol sequence: the prospective test, as v1's session C.
* Points that break a rule are redrawn (D: 0 rejected, E: 0).

Why these parameters: **delayInt** CDS integration window: white noise falls with a longer window, slow noise grows; the strongest noise knob; **delay_H** horizontal clock hold: charge transfer and horizontal clock-induced charge; **delayRGH** reset-gate hold before the pedestal window: settling of the reset; **VDD** output drain: the amplifier's operating point (noise, gain); **VR** reset drain: reset level and its noise; **RG_H** reset-gate high rail; **RG_L** reset-gate low rail; **SW_L** summing-well low rail; **H_low** horizontal clock low rails: clock-induced charge; **V_low** vertical clock low rails: clock-induced charge, dark current at the surface; **TG** transfer gate (high = low); **VSUB** substrate voltage: depletion, dark current.

## Packet procedure (daq_scripts/manual_packet.sh, 2026-11-03)

1. reset to nominal and read back; flush;
2. 5 reference trace sets (4 channels, each board separately, 2 s pause after every trace); flush;
3. the changes; 2 settling traces (not used);
4. 10 trace sets at the new settings; flush;
5. the image (img.toml), 2 s pause; standalone; flush; reset to nominal and read back.

## Before taking it (to decide)

* the number of trace sets: whether 3 + 5 sets predict as well as 5 + 10 (checked on the first data; `--n-ref` / `--n-new` rewrite this plan);
* the image: 16 rows give the total lambda to ~2 % but its dark-current part only to ~11 %; 32 rows would measure that part ~3x better for ~5 more minutes per packet (an image_params TOML with NROW = 32);
* record the CCD temperature per packet (the drift of lambda), if a readout exists;
* the voltage windows (`reviewed = false`).
