## Image or WADERs scripts? (added 2026-10-16)

The same three board-101 images through the older route, panaSKImg with one hand-written JSON (`img_9.json` with
the planned geometry: ccd_rows 16, ccd_cols 852, id_row_start 0):

| | ch0 σe | ch1 σe | ch3 σe | fits with the gain locked at ~2x (2e- peak taken as 1e-) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hand JSON, raw images | 0.178, **0.100**, 0.188 | 0.178, 0.200, **0.104** | 0.233, 0.243, 0.236 | 2 of 9, **no flag raised** |
| hand JSON, trend removed | **0.090**, 0.162, 0.161 | 0.169, 0.167, 0.169 | 0.215, 0.211, **0.112** | 2 of 9, no flag |
| run_WADERs, raw images | 0.182, 0.197, 0.191 | 0.182, 0.202, 0.199 | 0.225, 0.244, 0.237 | 0 of 9 |
| run_WADERs, trend removed | 0.172, 0.162, 0.165 | 0.171, 0.170, 0.172 | 0.215, 0.215, 0.213 | 0 of 9 |

* **The along-row baseline is in the images**, not in the scripts: it is measured directly on the pixels without any
  fit, the raw skips (`mb_L1`) show the start-of-pixel transient before any analysis, both WADERs routes read σe ~12 %
  high on the raw images and agree once the trend is removed, and the effect scales with 1/NDCM (10-06 image).
* **comm_scripts' run_WADERs helps**: it never mis-identified the electron peaks (the hand JSON did in 4 of 18 fits,
  silently: σe halved, λ ~10x low), it sets the geometry from the header, and its diagnostics (χ²/ndf, errors scaled by
  √χ²/ndf, DC bias against the 0e-/1e- counts: −0.3 to −0.4 for ch3 on the raw images) pointed at the problem. On
  simulated images with a known truth it was within 0–4 % where the hand JSON was 8–12 % off.
* Its limits here: the dead-channel test is unreliable on 16-row images (keep `--keep-dead`); the quoted σe errors (1.7 %)
  are smaller than the repeat scatter on raw images (4–6 %); its reference leaves out the first 2 rows (2 of 16); and no
  per-row pedestal method can remove a baseline that drifts along the row — the trend removal (or NDCMPRE at acquisition)
  is needed for that.
